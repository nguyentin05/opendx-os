 1. Go modules compiled into the project's binaries
 .
 Go links statically, so these modules are part of the distributed binaries
 and container images. Their full license and NOTICE texts are in
 THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt, generated by
 scripts/third_party_notices.py. The last column names the module directory
 whose commands link them ("operator" covers dxos-operator, dxos-controller and
 dxos-knowledge-dispatcher; "capabilities" covers dxos-authz, cap-process,
 cap-hr and cap-data).

 6. Python AI runtime dependencies
 .
 THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt records the locked runtime distributions, their
 wheel license declarations where available, and the license and NOTICE files
 found in the installed wheels. Entries marked UNVERIFIED require review.
 .

 extension. The Psycopg wheels declare LGPL-3.0-only. Image distributors must
 retain the required notices, provide the LGPL and GPL texts, and fulfill
 corresponding-source obligations for the library binaries they convey.
 The combined work must allow use with a modified, interface-compatible
 library under LGPL section 4 and allow reverse engineering to debug library
 modifications. Provide installation information where required.

 exact corresponding source and notices for the bundled binaries. See
