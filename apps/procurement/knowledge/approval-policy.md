# Procurement approval guidance

A requester creates and submits a purchase request. The department manager
reviews requests from the requester's department, and finance reviews the budget
after department approval. A reviewer does not approve their own request.

This example describes the application's declared workflow. Reading this guidance
does not authorize a command or bypass its current role and record checks.
